Ever wondered why the bugs look pretty much the same wherever you go?
 Or why a flamethrower works perfectly well against creatures that 
 literally live inside a volcano?
 If that doesn't make you question their evolutionary choices — or your 
 own existence — this difficulty might.

This is a biome-specific difficulty built around environmental 
 adaptation. Every biome becomes its own unique challenge, featuring
 biome-specific enemy variants, resistances and weaknesses based on the
 environment they inhabit.
 What works wonders in one biome might barely scratch the bugs in 
 another.

So check your destination. Check your loadout. Then get to work, miner!

 Rock and Stone!

---------------------------------------------------------------------------

Features:

- Unique Enemy Pools for every Biome and Sabotage Missions

- Enemies have different Resistance Values based on the habitat they're
  living in 
	-> there is a table in the diff's description

- Fully reskinned Enemies

- 9 Difficulties in one
